## What I am shipping

### [EchoType](https://github.com/Talljack/echo-type) — master English through real content

One learning workspace for listening, speaking, reading, and writing. Import an article, YouTube video, file, or audio and turn it into an immersive practice loop with AI tutoring, pronunciation feedback, CEFR assessment, and spaced repetition.

[**Try the web app →**](https://echo-type.app) &nbsp; [**Download the desktop app →**](https://github.com/Talljack/echo-type/releases)

`Next.js 16` `React 19` `TypeScript` `Tauri 2` `Vercel AI SDK` `Supabase`

### [WhereMyMoney](https://github.com/Talljack/subscription-manage-community) — see where every recurring payment goes

A subscription intelligence workspace for tracking recurring services, renewal dates, and multi-currency spending in one place. Reminders, visual analytics, annual reports, and an AI financial assistant make hidden recurring costs easier to understand and act on.

[**Open WhereMyMoney →**](https://my-subscriptions.vercel.app) &nbsp; [**Join the community →**](https://github.com/Talljack/subscription-manage-community)

`Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Drizzle ORM` `Stripe` `Resend` `AI Assistant`

### [Menu Hub](https://github.com/Talljack/menu-hub) — take back your macOS menu bar

A native, keyboard-first menu bar manager built with Swift 6, SwiftUI, and AppKit. Search, group, and activate menu bar items without Screen Recording, analytics, a cloud service, or an account. Available in 10 languages for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

[**Download Menu Hub →**](https://github.com/Talljack/menu-hub/releases/latest)

`Swift 6` `SwiftUI` `AppKit` `macOS 14+` `Privacy-first`

## How I build

- **Product first:** start from a real workflow, ship the smallest useful loop, then learn from use.
- **Local and private by default:** keep data on-device whenever the product allows it.
- **Native where it matters:** use Swift/AppKit for macOS behavior and Tauri for lightweight cross-platform apps.
- **Open by default:** document the trade-offs, publish reusable parts, and make contribution paths clear.
